Build a project with friends and an AI. Make a room for your GitHub repo, run its AI on your own computer (your Claude login, an API key, or a local Ollama model), and invite people in.

    How a room works

    1. You create the room, so you own it.
    2. On your computer, run the gitclaudehub host command the room shows you. Every turn runs there, with your AI and your git access. This site never sees your keys.
    3. Send friends the invite link and the room password. Everyone watches each turn live, approves commands, and every turn is committed to your repo.
    4. As the owner you can lock the room, approve commands yourself, remove people, and delete the room.
